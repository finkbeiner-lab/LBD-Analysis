LBD
==============================

Analyze data of LBD disease through AI pathology

Lewy Body Dementia disease has two subtypes - Parkinson's Disease with Dementia (PDD) and Dementia with Lewy Bodies (DLB). Distinguishing these subtypes will help in developing better therapeutics.

Goal is to distinguish brains of PDD and DLB patients.

Three complementary methods are developed

Method 1 Supervised learning

Method 2 Slide level graph learning

Method 3 Crop level learning
